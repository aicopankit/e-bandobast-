Frequently Asked Questions - FAQs for the 78th Annual Nirankari Sant Samagam
General Information and Samagam Programme
When and where is the 78th Nirankari Sant Samagam taking place?
The 78
th
Annual Nirankari Sant Samagam will be held in the esteemed presence of Satguru Mata Sudiksha Ji Maharaj at the Sant Nirankari Adhyatmik Sthal, Samalkha (Haryana) from October 31 to November 3, 2025 followed by Guru Vandana on November 04, 2025.
What is the complete schedule of Events?
Visit
78
th
Nirankari Sant Samagam – Sant Nirankari Mission
for complete details of the programme and other Samagam related information.
When will the Sewadal Rally take place?
The Sewadal Rally will take place on Sunday November 1, 2025, from 11:00 A.M. to 01:00 P.M. in the main Samagam Pandal.
When and where will the General Body Meeting be held?
The General Body Meeting will take place on Monday, November 3, 2025, from 12:00 noon to 01:00 P.M. in the main Samagam Pandal. Please arrive by 10:00 A.M. to ensure a timely start.
When and where will the Gyan Pracharaks Meeting be held?
The Gyan Pracharaks Meeting will take place on Thursday, October 30, 2025, from 04:00 P.M. to 06:00 P.M. in the main Samagam Pandal. Please arrive by 03:30 P.M. to ensure a timely start.
When and where will the Meeting of Bal Satsang; Mahila Satsang; and English Medium Satsang Incharges be held?
The Meeting of Bal Sangat; Mahila Satsang; and English Medium Satsang Incharges will take place on Sunday, November 2, 2025, from 09:00 A.M. to 11:00 A.M. in the main Samagam Pandal.
When and where will the Khetriya Sanchalaks Meeting be held?
The Khetriya Sanchalaks Meeting will take place on Tuesday, October 28, 2025, from 11:00 A.M. to 01:00 P.M. in the main Samagam Pandal. Sewadal Sanchalak, Sewadal Sanchalika shall also be a part of this meeting.
When is the Kavi Sammelan scheduled?
No Kavi Sammelan this year.
What is Guru Vandana, and when will it take place?
Guru Vandana, a devotional program honoring the Satguru, will occur on Tuesday, November 4, 2025, from 6:00 P.M. onwards. It will include spiritual insights and heartfelt expressions of devotion.
Will there be any Mass Marriages event around Samagam?
The Mass Marriages ceremony will be held at Sant Nirankari Adhyatmik Sthal, Samalkha (Haryana) on November 6, 2025 (Thursday) at 11:00 A.M. (IST) onwards.
All the saints who wish to avail this opportunity may kindly contact the Social Welfare Vibhag of Sant Nirankari Mandal through local Mukhi/ Sanyojak/ Zonal Incharge and may kindly complete the formalities well in advance.
More details about Mass Marriages may be found at
https://www.nirankari.org/news/massmarriages20251106/
Arrangements At Samagam
What arrangements are made for attendees at the Samagam grounds?
The Samagam site is divided into various grounds for convenience:
Ground A: Main Satsang Pandal area (adjacent to GT Road)
Ground B: Left side behind Ground A
Ground C: Right side behind Ground A
Ground D: Behind Ground C, adjacent to Bodwal Majri railway station
Devotees visiting the Samagam Grounds will have facilities of 24×7 food in canteens, community lodging, arrangements, health and medical support during the 4-day Annual Nirankari Sant Samagam.
What are the Langar timings?
Langar will be served from 09:00 A.M. to 07:00 P.M. and after the Discourse of the Satguru until 11:30 P.M.
What are the dining arrangements during the Samagam?
Apart from the Langar (community meals), all grounds will have canteens offering refreshments such as milk, tea, coffee, Biscuts, soft drinks, fruits, etc.
Is there any accommodation facility in Nirankari Colony during Samagam days?
There is no facility for staying at Sant Nirankari Colony, Delhi, from October 21 to November 15, 2025. Satguru Mata Ji will be available for darshan at the Samagam site itself.
Can I stay in Nirankari Colony?
No, There is no accommodation arrangements from October 21 to November 15, 2025 at Nirankari Colony, Delhi.
Can I visit Nirankari Sarovar in Burari (near Nirankari Colony) during Samagam days?
Yes. Nirankari Sarovar will remain open from 07:00 A.M. to 04:00 P.M. during the samagam days
How can I return home urgently from the highway?
Saints can board roadway buses from the temporary bus stops, which will travel towards Delhi or Chandigarh.
Are there any special arrangements for persons with special needs?
Yes, special arrangements will be available for Divayang (Persons with special needs) to ensure a comfortable spiritual experience.
Who can access the reserved enclosure at the Samagam?
The reserved enclosure with limited and secure seating is available only for specially invited guests and representatives from distant regions. For all other attendees, seating will be available in designated blocks on either side of the central Pandal area.
What are the general Guidelines for Devotees Visiting the Samagam Grounds?
1. All attendees are encouraged to maintain decorum in lines for Namaskar, Langar, canteens, bathrooms, and exhibitions giving preference to elders, senior citizens and saints requiring special assistances. Please cooperate with Sewadal members and adhere to the standards of cleanliness.
2.
Given the current global security concerns, all attendees are requested to follow these guidelines:
Avoid touching any unattended items.
Report any suspicious items to the Samagam Committee or Sewadal Office immediately.
Refrain from bringing unnecessary valuables.
Cooperate with Sewadal members and local police on security duty.
3. Attendees are requested to ensure cleanliness in the following areas:
Toilets
Bathrooms
Drinking water stations
Residential tents
Canteens
Railway station premises
Around waste bins
4.
The rights to all photography or other materials related to the Sant Nirankari Mission are reserved by the Sant Nirankari Mandal. Devotees and attendees are requested not to take photographs; Saints may visit the official stalls managed by the Publication Department and buy a wide range of publications and photographs.
5.
Devotees from Delhi/Greater Delhi are requested to give preference to the devotees from far off places to seek Satguru Darshan and refrain from joining the Namaskar lines.
18. What Internet and telecommunication facilities are available at the Samagam?
Live Webcast:
The Samagam will be live-streamed on
live.nirankari.org
, allowing those unable to attend to view the sessions from anywhere.
Helpline Number
: A toll-free central helpline (1800-123-2005) will be available from September 22 to October 20, 2025, from 09:00 A.M. to 09:00 P.M., and 24 hours from October 22 to November 07, 2025.
WhatsApp Chatbot:
From September 29 to November 07, 2025, an automated chatbot will be available on WhatsApp at +91 926 662 9848 to provide information on live broadcasts, timings, location, transport, and general inquiries.
Local Inquiries:
Attendees on the Samagam grounds can contact local inquiry centers or the Sewadal Office for assistance.
Transportation
To whom should we contact for availing transport service?
Volunteers will record your details and share them with the transport department. Please ensure to capture your details in the URL provided.
Are there any specific instructions for railway travel to the Samagam?
A 2-minute stop for some designated trains at the Bhoddwal Majri railway station is authorized from October 06, 2025, to November 07, 2025. Additional trains will not stop at this station. Attendees are encouraged to disembark here instead of Panipat station. A reception area at this Railway station will guide attendees to their tents.
Are there assistance booths for attendees at various railway stations?
Yes, help desks will be available at key stations: Delhi, New Delhi, Hazrat Nizamuddin, Subzi Mandi, Sarai Rohilla, Anand Vihar (Ghaziabad), Bhodwal Majri, and Panipat.
How to reach Samagam Grounds from Delhi and other nearby cities?
Like every year, drastic transportation arrangements have been made with the help of Indian Railways. The Transport Department has made transport arrangements to help saints coming from outside to reach the Samagam venue from all the arrival points at IGI airport, various stations including Old Delhi, New Delhi, Hazrat Nizamuddin, Anand Vihar,  Sarai Rohilla and Subzi Mandi, as also the Kashmere Gate, and Sarai Kale Khan ISBT. Besides, transport facility will be available to devotees getting down at Bhodwal Majri, Sonipat and Panipat Railway Stations also.
How should arrival and departure information be shared?
For proper arrangements, each visiting group must submit details, including the number of attendees, arrival date and time, and mode of transport, by October 15, 2025. Please send this information to:

Is Gyan Kaksh available on all grounds?
Yes, Gyan Kaksh is available in all grounds (except Ground No. A).
Will there be any facility to attain Brahm Gyan (Divine Knowledge)?
Yes, Brahm Gyan sessions will be set up across all ground (except Ground No. A) to allow new seekers to receive this spiritual knowledge.
Where are the Brahm Gyan Kakshs located?

What are the timings for Brahm Gyan Kaksh on Guru Vandana Divas?
On Guru Vandana, the Brahm Gyan Kaksh will open at 08:00 A.M. and will remain open until the Guru Vandana starts.
In which languages will Brahm Gyan be delivered?

Accounts Related
Will there be a facility to withdraw Cash from ATM in Samagam grounds?
ATMs will be installed in Ground A. Saints can make use of these ATMs. In grounds B, C and D, mobile ATM vans will be available from 10:00 A.M. to 05:00 P.M. at prominent points.
Can I deposit my donation towards Building Fund, Namaskar, Langar Sewa, Samagam Sewa etc. in the samagam grounds?
Accounts department of Sant Nirankari Mandal will set up 4 offices in each ground. You may deposit your donations there and receive its receipt as well.
Can I make donations to receive benefit under Section 80G of Income Tax Act?
Benefit of 80G is not available for donations made to Sant Nirankari Mandal
.
To get benefit of 80G, donations would need to be made to Sant Nirankari Charitable Foundation.  Rev. Shri Rohit Bajaj Ji may please be contacted at Mobile no. 8745001114 for any further information.
How much donation can be made in cash?
A maximum of Rs. 20,000 can be deposited in cash by any individual with proper address details. If the amount deposited under a single name in cash is more than Rs. 20,000, a copy of PAN Card must be provided. For deposits greater than 50,000, it is recommended to make the donations via a cheque.
Can I deposit my donation by Debit Card / Credit Card or UPI using Mobile Phone?
Yes. Donations can be deposited through any of these mediums. Swipe machines will be available at all Samagam Sewa counters.
Can I deposit my donation in Foreign Currency and get a receipt of it?
No. Foreign Currency in cash is not accepted for donation.
In the Central Accounts Office, in Ground A, an authorised bank officer will be available to exchange Foreign Currency with Indian Rupees.
After conversion, donations can be made normally in Indian Rupees for which a receipt will be provided.
Is there a facility for safekeeping of valuables and large cash?
Yes. A securities room providing such facility of safekeeping will be available in the Central Accounts Office in Ground A.
Can I make my donations through online banking channels?
Yes. For this you may visit the official website of Sant Nirankari Mandal and go to Donations Link. The URL of this link is
https://www.nirankari.org/contribute
Can I deposit my branch’s receipt book and the associated cash in the samagam grounds? Can I collect a new receipt book or accounts related forms in the samagam grounds?
Yes. You may kindly contact Central Accounts Office, in Ground A for any such service. You may also contact Rev. Gurmeet Ji (Mobile –

For any other Accounts related information, saints may kindly contact Rev. Vijay Batra Ji, Manager Accounts (Mobile – 9266629829).
Exhibition
What are the timings for the Nirankari Exhibition?
The exhibition will be open from 9:00 A.M. to Midnight from October 29 to November 4, 2025.
What can attendees expect from the Nirankari Exhibition?
The Nirankari Exhibition will showcase the Mission’s history, principles, and art. This year, It will be open daily from 9:00 a.m. to Midnight from October 29 to November 4. Additionally, there will be exhibitions focused on child welfare and social initiatives.
Magazine &amp; Publications
How can attendees access Nirankari publications and literature?
The Nirankari publications, including
Sant Nirankari
,
Ek Nazar
, and
Hansati Duniya
, will be available for subscription. A computerized setup will assist attendees with subscriptions in Ground A. Books and souvenirs, including
Divya Deeksha
;
Yug Nirmata
(collections of teachings by Satguru Mata Sudiksha Ji Maharaj and Mata Savinder Hardev Ji) and many more, will be available at the publication stalls.
Medical Facilities
44. Are there any emergency medical services available at the Samagam grounds?
Yes, adequate emergency medical services are available in Samagam Ground.
Allopathic Dispensaries with indoor facilities will remain functional in all grounds during 11-21 Nov, 2024
During 14-20 Nov. 2024, Ground A Dispensary will also have a 100-bed indoor facility along with a 10-bed ICU.
45. Is any ambulance service available for critical patients?
Yes. 40 ambulances will be positioned across various grounds to cater to any emergency situation.
46. Will there be any diagnostic facilities available for the patients
Yes, ECG and laboratory services will be available.
47. Will there be any arrangement for emergency dental services?
There will be arrangements for emergency and regular dental treatment.
48. Will homeopathic treatment be available?
Yes, homeopathic treatment will be provided on all grounds.
49. Are there any first aid facilities across the grounds?
Fourteen first aid posts will be operational across all grounds to provide basic treatment.
50. What additional therapies are available?
Physiotherapy, Sujok, Acupressure, and Chiropractic treatments will be accessible for devotees.
51. Will there be any other charitable services available in the dispensaries?
There will be a facility to provide free spectacles to the needy devotees.
52. I have old parents who want to attend the Samagam. Is it advisable to bring elderly or physically/mentally unwell individuals?
It is recommended to avoid bringing elderly individuals, those with serious health conditions, and pregnant women to prevent any discomfort.
