Frequently Asked Questions
79th Annual Nirankari Sant Samagam
&times;
General Information and Samagam Programme
1. When and where is the 79th Nirankari Sant Samagam taking place?
The 79th Annual Nirankari Sant Samagam will be held
in the esteemed presence of Satguru Mata Sudiksha Ji Maharaj
at the
Sant Nirankari Adhyatmik Sthal,
Samalkha (Haryana)
from
October 23 to October 26, 2026
,
followed by
Guru Vandana on October 27, 2026
.
2. What is the complete schedule of Events?
Please refer to the
official 79th Nirankari Sant Samagam
programme
for complete details of the schedule and
Samagam-related information.
3. When will the Sewadal Rally take place?
The Sewadal Rally will take place on
Saturday, October 24, 2026,
from 11:00 A.M. to 01:00 P.M.
in the main Samagam Pandal.
4. What is Guru Vandana, and when will it take place?
Guru Vandana, a devotional programme honouring
the Satguru, will take place on
Tuesday, October 27, 2026,
from 06:00 P.M. onwards.
Arrangements at Samagam
5. What arrangements are made for attendees at the Samagam grounds?
The Samagam site is divided into various grounds
for convenience:
Ground A:
Main Satsang Pandal area (adjacent to GT Road)
Ground B:
Left side behind Ground A
Ground C:
Right side behind Ground A
Ground D:
Behind Ground C, adjacent to Bhodwal Majri Railway Station
Devotees visiting the Samagam Grounds will have
facilities including food in canteens, community
lodging arrangements, health and medical support.
6. What are the Langar timings?
Langar will be served from
09:00 A.M. to 07:00 P.M.
and after the Discourse of the Satguru until
11:30 P.M.
7. What are the dining arrangements during the Samagam?
Apart from the Langar (community meals), all grounds
will have canteens offering refreshments such as milk,
tea, coffee, biscuits, soft drinks, fruits, etc.
8. Can I visit Nirankari Sarovar in Burari during Samagam days?
Yes. Nirankari Sarovar will remain open from
07:00 A.M. to 04:00 P.M.
during the Samagam days.
9. How can I return home urgently from the highway?
Saints can board roadway buses from the temporary
bus stops, which will travel towards Delhi or Chandigarh.
10. Are there any special arrangements for persons with special needs?
Yes. Special arrangements will be available for
Divyang (persons with special needs) to ensure
a comfortable spiritual experience.
11. Who can access the reserved enclosure at the Samagam?
The reserved enclosure with limited and secure seating
is available only for specially invited guests and
representatives from distant regions.
For all other attendees, seating will be available
in designated blocks on either side of the central
Pandal area.
12. What are the general guidelines for devotees visiting the Samagam Grounds?
Maintain decorum in lines for Namaskar, Langar,
canteens, bathrooms and exhibitions.
Give preference to elders, senior citizens and
devotees requiring special assistance.
Cooperate with Sewadal members and local police
personnel deployed for security.
Avoid touching unattended or suspicious items
and immediately report them.
Avoid bringing unnecessary valuables to the
Samagam Grounds.
Please help maintain cleanliness throughout
the Samagam Grounds.
Rights to photography and other materials related
to the Sant Nirankari Mission are reserved by
Sant Nirankari Mandal.
Internet and Telecommunication
13. What Internet and telecommunication facilities are available at the Samagam?
Live Webcast:
The Samagam will be live-streamed on
live.nirankari.org
.
Toll-Free Helpline:
1800-123-2005
WhatsApp Chatbot:
+91 926 662 9848
Transportation
14. To whom should we contact for availing transport service?
Volunteers will record your details and share them
with the Transport Department. Please ensure that
your travel details are submitted through the
prescribed channel/form.
15. Are there any specific instructions for railway travel to the Samagam?
Special railway arrangements have been made for
devotees travelling to the Samagam.
For the latest list of trains and stoppage information
for
Bhodwal Majri (BDMJ) Railway Station
,
please refer to the
official 79th Samagam information page
.
16. Are there assistance booths for attendees at various railway stations?
Yes. Help desks will be available at key railway
stations to assist devotees travelling to the Samagam.
17. How to reach Samagam Grounds from Delhi and other nearby cities?
Transportation arrangements are made to assist saints
arriving from outside Delhi through major railway
stations, bus terminals and the airport.
Transport assistance is generally available from major
arrival points including Delhi, New Delhi,
Hazrat Nizamuddin, Anand Vihar, Sarai Rohilla and
Subzi Mandi railway stations, as well as Kashmere Gate
and Sarai Kale Khan ISBT.
Assistance is also available for devotees arriving
at Bhodwal Majri, Sonipat and Panipat Railway Stations.
Gyan Kaksh
18. Is Gyan Kaksh available on all grounds?
Yes. Gyan Kaksh is available in all grounds
except Ground A
.
19. Will there be any facility to attain Brahm Gyan (Divine Knowledge)?
Yes. Brahm Gyan sessions will be available across
all grounds except Ground A, allowing new seekers
to receive spiritual knowledge.
20. Where are the Brahm Gyan Kakshs located?
Ground B:
Brahm Gyan Kaksh No. 1
Ground C:
Brahm Gyan Kaksh Nos. 2 &amp; 3
Ground D:
Brahm Gyan Kaksh No. 4
21. What are the timings for the Brahm Gyan Kakshs during the Samagam?
October 23, 2026
05:00 A.M. to 03:00 P.M.
09:00 P.M. to 12:00 Midnight
October 24, 2026
05:00 A.M. to 03:00 P.M.
09:00 P.M. to 12:00 Midnight
October 25, 2026
05:00 A.M. to 01:00 P.M.
09:00 P.M. to 12:00 Midnight
October 26, 2026
05:00 A.M. to 03:00 P.M.
09:00 P.M. to 12:00 Midnight
22. What are the timings for Brahm Gyan Kaksh on Guru Vandana Divas?
On
October 27, 2026 (Guru Vandana)
,
the Brahm Gyan Kaksh will open at
08:00 A.M.
and remain open until
Guru Vandana starts.
23. In which languages will Brahm Gyan be delivered?
Brahm Gyan will primarily be delivered in Hindi,
English, Marathi, Gujarati, Nepali, Tamil, Telugu,
Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Assamese and Oriya.
Accounts Related
24. Will there be a facility to withdraw cash from ATM in Samagam grounds?
ATMs will be installed in Ground A.
In Grounds B, C and D, mobile ATM vans will be
available from
10:00 A.M. to 05:00 P.M.
at prominent points.
25. Can I deposit my donation towards Building Fund, Namaskar, Langar Sewa, Samagam Sewa etc.?
The Accounts Department of Sant Nirankari Mandal
will set up offices at the Samagam Grounds.
Donations may be deposited there and receipts
will be provided.
26. Can I make donations to receive benefit under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act?
Benefit of 80G is not available for donations
made to Sant Nirankari Mandal.
To avail applicable 80G benefits, donations would
need to be made to Sant Nirankari Charitable Foundation.
27. Can I deposit my donation by Debit Card, Credit Card or UPI?
Yes. Donations can be deposited through these digital
payment modes. Swipe machines will be available at
Samagam Sewa counters.
28. Can I deposit my donation in Foreign Currency and get a receipt?
Foreign currency in cash is not accepted directly
for donation. At the Central Accounts Office in
Ground A, an authorised bank officer will be available
for currency exchange.
After conversion, donations may be made in Indian
Rupees, for which a receipt will be provided.
29. Is there a facility for safekeeping of valuables and large cash?
Yes. A securities room providing safekeeping facilities
will be available in the Central Accounts Office
in Ground A.
30. Can I make my donations through online banking channels?
Yes. Please use the relevant official contribution page:
Sant Nirankari Mandal
Sant Nirankari Charitable Foundation
Exhibition
31. What can attendees expect from the Nirankari Exhibition?
The Nirankari Exhibition will showcase the Mission's
history, principles and art. Exhibitions focusing on
child welfare and social initiatives will also be available.
Magazine &amp; Publications
32. How can attendees access Nirankari publications and literature?
Nirankari publications including
Sant Nirankari
,
Ek Nazar
and
Hansati Duniya
will be available for subscription.
Books, souvenirs and other Mission publications
will also be available at designated publication stalls.
Medical Facilities
33. Are there any emergency medical services available at the Samagam grounds?
Yes. Emergency medical services and dispensary
facilities will be available at the Samagam Grounds.
34. Is any ambulance service available for critical patients?
Yes. Ambulances will be positioned across various
grounds to cater to emergency situations.
35. Will there be any diagnostic facilities available for patients?
Yes. ECG and laboratory services will be available.
36. Will there be any arrangement for emergency dental services?
Yes. Arrangements will be available for emergency
and regular dental treatment.
37. Will homeopathic treatment be available?
Yes. Homeopathic treatment will be provided
on the Samagam grounds.
38. Are there any first aid facilities across the grounds?
Yes. First aid posts will be operational across
the grounds to provide basic treatment.
39. What additional therapies are available?
Physiotherapy, Sujok, Acupressure and Chiropractic
treatments will be accessible for devotees.
40. Will there be any other charitable services available in the dispensaries?
There will be a facility to provide free spectacles
to needy devotees.
41. I have old parents who want to attend the Samagam. Is it advisable to bring elderly or physically/mentally unwell individuals?
It is recommended to avoid bringing elderly individuals,
persons with serious health conditions and pregnant
women if attending the Samagam may cause them discomfort.
